# Paper examples and source coordinates

The five examples selected from the Tauri benchmark archive are the TorsionNet500 benzimidazole–indazole conformer and four QCell structures (sugar, ion with water, single lipid and DNA dinucleotide). The catalog records the selected archive members and frame numbers. Coordinates are in ångströms. [Structure catalog](../web/paper-catalog.json) · [Tauri dataset](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22920714) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

## Upstream benchmark credits

The external interaction datasets are JSCH-2005 by Jurečka et al. and PLA15 by Kříž and Řezáč. [JSCH-2005](https://doi.org/10.1039/b600027d) · [PLA15](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.9b01171)

The conformer comes from TorsionNet500 by Rai et al. The deposited TorsionNet500 reference energies were recomputed at PBE0+MBD. [TorsionNet500](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.1c01346)

The sugar, lipid, DNA and ion examples originate from QCell. [QCell](https://doi.org/10.1088/3050-287X/ae5267)
